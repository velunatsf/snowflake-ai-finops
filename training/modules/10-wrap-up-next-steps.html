The Three FinOps Habits for AI

Habit 1: ESTIMATE

"Test on 10 rows. Project to full dataset. Decide."

Never run AI at scale without first testing on a small sample, measuring the cost, and projecting to your full dataset size.

Habit 2: TAG & BUDGET

"Cost centers first. AI feature budgets second."

Tag users, then attach AI Functions / Agents / CoWork / CoCo to tag-scoped budgets so spend is attributed before it surprises finance.

Habit 3: MONITOR & CHOOSE

"Dashboard weekly. Cheapest model that meets quality."

Watch SERVICE_TYPE breakouts for Agents/CoWork/CoCo. Prefer specialized AISQL and Dynamic Model Routing before premium models.

C

Why Cortex AI Rewrites the FinOps Rules

Traditional FinOps focuses on warehouse compute. But AI workloads break four fundamental assumptions. These principles (covered in depth in Module 08) explain why dedicated AI monitoring is essential.

01 - Credit Burn

AI credit consumption scales faster than traditional compute. Thousands of COMPLETE/SUMMARIZE/EXTRACT calls burn credits silently - no warehouse to suspend.

02 - Behavior

AI workloads are experimental and hard to predict. Teams switch models frequently and run exploratory prompts at scale. Usage patterns shift weekly.

03 - Visibility

Serverless AI spend is invisible to warehouse dashboards. Cortex AI runs outside warehouses - traditional monitoring misses it entirely.

04 - Signals

Token counts alone don't tell the full story. The same prompt on a large model costs 10x more than on a lighter one. Without tracking model choice, costs stay hidden.

AI spend will grow, so govern it now

FinOps for AI is now a top priority for platform teams. Govern AISQL, Agents, CoWork, and CoCo with tag-based budgets and usage views before spend scales - the teams who govern it now will be the ones who can afford to grow it later.

Take-Home Cheat Sheet: The Seven Views

ACCOUNT_USAGE View Tracks Key Columns
CORTEX_AI_FUNCTIONS_USAGE_HISTORY AISQL functions (COMPLETE, SENTIMENT, ...) CREDITS, FUNCTION_NAME, MODEL_NAME
CORTEX_AISQL_USAGE_HISTORY AISQL token metering rollups TOKEN_CREDITS, TOKENS, QUERY_TAG
CORTEX_AGENT_USAGE_HISTORY Cortex Agents Tokens / credits per agent request
SNOWFLAKE_COWORK_USAGE_HISTORY Snowflake CoWork (org-level view; SERVICE_TYPE may still be SNOWFLAKE_INTELLIGENCE) TOKENS_GRANULAR (input, output, cache_read_input, cache_write_input), CREDITS_GRANULAR
SNOWFLAKE_COCO_USAGE_HISTORY Unified CoCo (CLI + Desktop + Snowsight); interface-specific views also exist TOKEN_CREDITS, TOKENS_GRANULAR (cache fields), USER_NAME
CORTEX_SEARCH_DAILY_USAGE_HISTORY Search indexing & queries CREDITS, SERVICE_NAME, CONSUMPTION_TYPE
METERING_DAILY_HISTORY Aggregated daily metering by SERVICE_TYPE CREDITS_BILLED, SERVICE_TYPE

Latency varies by view (~2 to 5 min for AI Functions usage history; ~45 min for many ACCOUNT_USAGE views). Budget refresh: 6.5 hr default, 1 hr low-latency. This table is a cheat sheet. The full explanation of why these views are split, plus every runnable query, is in Module 06. Cache KPIs are in Module 07 and budget DDL is in Module 08.
