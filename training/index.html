Why FinOps for AI

FinOps for AI is not just about dashboards. It maps business value to every token spent. AI breaks the traditional cloud cost playbook because token consumption is volatile, nonlinear, and growing rapidly. Effective FinOps for AI optimizes the outcome of each token, connects consumption to measurable business value, and asks whether every token earns what it costs. This is the shift from Token Economics (Tokconomics) to Valuemaxxing.
